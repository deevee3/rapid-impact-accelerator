SPEAKER NOTES (SCRIPT)

Slide 0 — Title
Welcome to The DIY System Mini-Course: Turn Your Profile Into a Magnet in 30 Minutes. The promise is independence—so you can update your profile as you grow, keep it algorithm-friendly, and protect your edge long term.

Slide 1 — What You’ll Learn
By the end, you’ll choose clear positioning, build a focused keyword map, write a headline and About that convert, align Experience and Featured for credibility, and track simple metrics to iterate confidently.

Slide 2 — Why Positioning First
If your profile tries to be everything to everyone, it becomes nothing to anyone. Positioning is the decision that makes every other decision simpler. We start here so every word after this pulls in the same direction.

Slide 3 — One-Line Positioning Formula
Use one of these to create your one-liner.
Consultants: I help [WHO] achieve [RESULT] without [PAIN] via [MECHANISM], backed by [PROOF].
Job-seekers: [TITLE] who [RESULT/PROOF] in [INDUSTRY], now targeting [ROLES] to deliver [OUTCOME].

Slide 4 — Define the Pieces
WHO is your specific audience. RESULT is a business outcome. PAIN is what they’re avoiding. MECHANISM is how you deliver results. PROOF are your numbers, brands, certifications, or years.

Slide 5 — Examples (Snapshot)
Examples:
- B2B SaaS copywriter: I help founders lift demo bookings 30–60% without more ad spend through conversion-focused email and landing pages; wins at Gong and Paddle.
- Clinical research ops: I help research sites activate trials faster and reduce deviations through lean SOPs and training; 17 processes implemented across Phase II–IV.
- PM fintech: Senior PM who 2x’d activation for 1.2M-user app; targeting risk/onboarding to reduce false positives and approve more good users.

Slide 6 — Decide and Commit (90 Days)
Choose one WHO for 90 days, one flagship RESULT you can defend, one MECHANISM you can explain simply, and list three PROOF points. Clarity beats breadth.

Slide 7 — Action + Pitfalls
Action: Write your one-liner and three proof bullets now. Pitfalls: vagueness, laundry lists of services, and zero proof. Tighten until it fits in one breath.

Slide 8 — Why Keywords
Keywords tell the platform who you are and who should find you. Done right, you’ll consistently appear in searches from the right people.

Slide 9 — Harvest in 10–15 Minutes
Pull five job posts or client offers and five strong profiles. Note repeated titles, skills, tools, industries, and outcomes. This is your vocabulary source.

Slide 10 — Build a 3-Tier Map (15 terms)
Tier 1 are your primary identifiers. Tier 2 are core skills, tools, or outcomes. Tier 3 are context terms like industry and use cases. Aim for 3–5, 5–7, and 3–5 terms respectively.

Slide 11 — Placement Strategy
Put 1–2 Tier 1 terms and one Tier 2 term in your headline. Use Tier 1 in your first two About lines and distribute Tier 2–3 naturally across About, titles, bullets, Skills, and Featured labels.

Slide 12 — Rules + Action
Write for humans first, use synonyms your audience uses, and review quarterly. Action: Build your 15-term map and highlight the 3–4 terms you’ll prioritize up top.

Slide 13 — First 80 Characters Matter
Your headline is the first filter. On mobile, only the first ~80 characters show. Front-load value so the right people immediately recognize you’re relevant.

Slide 14 — Pick a Proven Formula
Choose one proven structure: Role + ICP + Outcome; Outcome + Mechanism + Proof; Title | Keywords | Niche; I help WHO do WHAT; or Role + Category + Credibility.

Slide 15 — Make It Punchy
Use outcome verbs, include numbers or named brands when you can, and avoid vague buzzwords. Specifics create credibility and clicks.

Slide 16 — Examples (Snapshot)
Examples:
- Fractional CMO for B2B Startups → Pipeline in 90 Days | Ex-Adobe
- Clinical Research Ops Consultant | Faster Activation, Fewer Deviations
- Technical Writer | DevTools | Docs That Cut Support Tickets 30%

Slide 17 — Test and Iterate
If Search Appearances and Profile Views don’t rise 20–30% within 7 days, rotate one of your alternate headline drafts. Small changes can produce big differences.

Slide 18 — Action
Draft three headlines now. Publish your strongest and save two for testing next week.

Slide 19 — Treat It Like a Landing Page
Your About should quickly explain who you help, how you help, why you’re credible, and what to do next. Keep it scannable.

Slide 20 — 5-Part Structure
The structure: Hook, Snapshot, Value Proposition, Proof bullets, and CTA. This balances clarity with persuasion.

Slide 21 — Fill-In Template (Overview)
Use the template:
Hook: Most [WHO] struggle with [PAIN]. I help them achieve [OUTCOME] without [PAIN #2].
Snapshot: Over [X] years with [BRANDS/SECTORS], I’ve delivered [RESULTS].
Method: My approach: [STEP 1], [STEP 2], [STEP 3]—so you get [BENEFIT].
Proof bullets: three CAR results with numbers.
CTA: What to do next and how to reach you.

Slide 22 — Example: Consultant (SaaS)
Example:
Hook: Most PLG SaaS teams struggle to convert trials. I turn onboarding and lifecycle messages into revenue without more ad spend.
Snapshot: 6+ years, wins at Paddle and YC startups.
Method: Audit journey, rebuild messages around JTBD, A/B test key steps.
Proof: 9% → 17% trials-to-paid in 90 days; -22% churn; +38% demo bookings.
CTA: Message “PLAN” for a 3-step roadmap.

Slide 23 — Example: Clinical Research Ops
Example:
Hook: Timelines slip when SOPs are unclear. I enable faster activation and fewer deviations without burning out staff.
Snapshot: 8+ years; 17 processes across Phase II–IV.
Method: Map critical path, simplify SOPs/training, install quality checks.
Proof: -28% activation time; -35% deviations; audit readiness boosted.
CTA: Message “ACTIVATE” for a checklist.

Slide 24 — Example: Job-Seeking PM
Example:
Hook: Great fintech products die in onboarding. I design flows that approve more good users and cut false positives.
Snapshot: 7+ years; 1.2M MAU flows shipped.
Method: Diagnose friction, progressive KYC, align risk + product.
Proof: 2x activation; -31% false positives; SDK adopted by 12 partners.
CTA: Open to Senior PM roles in risk/payments; contact info.

Slide 25 — Micro-About + Tips
Create a 5-sentence micro version for skimmers. Write in first person, keep paragraphs short, and prefer numbers over adjectives. Put contact info and CTA at the end.

Slide 26 — Action
Draft your full About and a micro version now. Add your contact details and a clear CTA.

Slide 27 — Align Everything
Your Experience, Featured, Skills, and Settings should all reflect your positioning and keyword map. This is where you prove it.

Slide 28 — Titles + CAR Bullets
Add context to titles to match your niche. Write 3–5 bullets per role using CAR: lead with the Result, then Action and relevant tool or method.

Slide 29 — Featured and Proof
Pin three assets that prove your claims—case studies, portfolio samples, top posts. Label them with keyword-rich outcomes.

Slide 30 — Skills, Recs, Settings
Pin the three skills that match your target. Request two recommendations with a prompt that tees up a specific result. Fix your URL, contact visibility, and relevant toggles.

Slide 31 — Action
Update titles and bullets for your last two roles, add three Featured items, pin skills, adjust settings, and send two recommendation requests.

Slide 32 — Toolkit (Downloads)
You’ll get worksheets and templates for positioning, keyword mapping, headlines, About, CAR bullets, and a metrics tracker, so you can implement quickly and reuse the system.

Slide 33 — 24-Hour Sprint Plan
Hour 0: Capture baseline metrics and export your profile. Hour 1: Positioning + proof. Hour 2: Keyword map. Hour 3: Headline and micro-About. Hour 4: Experience, Skills, Settings, Featured. Hour 5 optional: post an update to spark visits.

Slide 34 — 7-Day Momentum Plan
Daily for 10 minutes: thoughtful comments on five target posts and add ten aligned connections with a short note. Share one win this week and pin it. On day seven, review metrics and iterate headline or About intro if needed.

Slide 35 — QA Checklist
Confirm headline clarity in the first 80 characters, at least three proof points, natural keyword placement, Featured assets that prove claims, visible CTA and contact info, and mobile-friendly readability.

Slide 36 — Closing
You now have a repeatable system: position, map keywords, write to outcomes, prove with evidence, and tune weekly. Iterate with data, not guesswork. You’re in control of your profile—and the opportunities it attracts.
